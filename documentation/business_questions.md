# Business Questions

This Power BI project is designed to answer questions such as:

1. How much revenue has been won?
2. How much revenue is currently in the sales pipeline?
3. What is the overall opportunity close rate?
4. How many opportunities are open, won, or lost?
5. Which products generate the most revenue?
6. Which territories perform best?
7. Which industries contribute the strongest opportunities?
8. Which sales owners perform best?
9. How does sales performance change over time?
10. What is the average won deal size?
11. How is actual/forecast performance tracking against the revenue goal?
12. What factors influence whether an opportunity is won?
13. What factors influence the number of days an opportunity remains open?
14. What factors are associated with discount percentage?
15. What underlying dimensions contribute most to sales performance?
